02 — PURPOSE

Guided by progress
grounded in place.

✦

OUR MISSION

Empowering MSMEs. Enabling Innovation. Building Sustainable Growth.

To establish and strengthen a network of local businesses in Batticaloa by providing MSMEs with the technology, knowledge, resources, and opportunities they need to innovate, overcome challenges, and achieve sustainable growth. We aim to empower entrepreneurs, create meaningful employment, and contribute to the long-term economic development of the region.

OUR VISION

Thriving businesses create thriving communities.

To transform Batticaloa into a thriving, technology-driven business ecosystem where empowered MSMEs and innovative enterprises create sustainable economic growth, generate employment, and contribute to the region’s long-term prosperity.

06 — OUR STORY

Rooted in a lived
understanding of need.

From the experiences of a place to a shared vision for its future.

EARLY YEARS

Growing up in Batticaloa

We saw the inequalities in access to education, technology, resources and opportunity — and formed a belief that every individual should have the chance to learn, innovate and build a better future.

THE INTERNET

Discovering new possibilities

Technology revealed a world that could connect people, create opportunity and become a powerful tool for social and economic change.

CHALLENGES

Building resilience

Conflict, the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami, the 2019 Easter attacks and economic hardship reinforced the need for a diverse, resilient economy built on new industries.

2019

SPM Industries is founded

A conviction that a strong startup and MSME ecosystem can drive economic development became a practical beginning.

TODAY

An ecosystem in motion

SPM Technologies, Academy, Renewables and Foundation work across technology, education, energy and social development — supporting people from ideas to opportunity.

THE FUTURE

A thriving, sustainable Batticaloa

Our journey has only begun. We invite entrepreneurs, innovators, businesses, investors and partners to build the future with us.
